## The marketing site and enquiries

The public pages under `src/app/[locale]/(marketing)/` stay static (seven marketing plus the four legal ones from spec 0015): nothing on that path reads `searchParams`, `headers()` or `cookies()`, the routes and their German slugs live in `PATHNAMES` and `MARKETING_ROUTES` (`src/i18n/pathnames.ts`), and every page carries `marketingMetadata`, an `opengraph-image.tsx` beside it and a `JsonLd` block. Copy is `marketing.*` keys in both catalogs; a price is `PACKAGES` in `src/features/marketing/packages.ts` and a contact fact is `SITE` in `site.ts` (a placeholder goes on `SITE_PLACEHOLDERS`, which a test keeps empty). Both buyers are in play (the safety manager and the CFO), and since the client review of 2026-09-11 (spec 0009, fifth amendment) the headline leads with the benchmark, "Benchmark your risks. Find your savings. Fix it – once.", and the franc figure is the proof beneath it, on the marketing pages and in their metadata alike. The dashboard heading and the email subject already lead with the benchmark, so the later pass they were held for is closed with no change. The expert register at `/expert-network/directory` is a static build artifact: `scripts/build-register.mts` behind `pnpm register:build` maps the SGAS export to `register.json`, dropping the address, phone, email and website columns in the script so they never enter the repo, and `register.ts` holds the pure filter and count functions the page and its tests share. A page that renders a grouped four digit number in a client component defers it until mount, because ICU groups it differently on Node and in the browser and the hydration mismatch leaves every control on that component inert. `submitEnquiry` in `src/features/marketing/actions.ts` runs the honeypot, the mount timing check and two counted rate limits, stores the address as a SHA 256 hash and then, never losing the row, fires the `enquiry.received` alert, the `enquiry_received` acknowledgement and the `enquiry_sent` event; ops work the rows in `src/features/enquiries/` and `purge-enquiries` handles retention. Browser code reads public variables through `@/lib/env.public` (zod must not reach the browser) and Sentry loads after the page in `src/instrumentation-client.ts`, both enforced by the Biome override; `pnpm build && pnpm budget` is the gate. The per environment checklist (owner facts, `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`, the schedule, the webhook, Rich Results, Lighthouse, production robots) lives in the runbook.
