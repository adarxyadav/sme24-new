# SME24 homepage — concept 01

Design and copy proposal, 9 September 2026. Review artifact only. No application routes, components, catalogs, or design tokens changed.

## Positioning

For Swiss business leaders and people responsible for workplace safety: understand the risk, put an estimated CHF value on it, and choose a defined engagement with a senior EHS expert. The free benchmark is the entry point. Expert judgment is the service. Fixed scope and visible prices reduce the buying uncertainty.

The first draft is in English. German should be written as a natural Swiss German-market adaptation after the direction is agreed, then reviewed in the same layouts.

## Art direction

Borrow the product clarity and disciplined hierarchy of Linear, with SME24's own industrial identity. A white, centered typographic hero leads directly into an inspectable example of the output. One existing photograph introduces the human expertise behind the service. The final black section closes the argument.

- Palette: pure white #FFFFFF, jet #000000, obsidian #1A1A1A, secondary ink #595959, hairline #E5E5E5, quiet surface #F7F7F7. These are mockup values; implementation maps to the existing semantic tokens.
- Type: existing Geist throughout. Display 72/74 at desktop, 40/42 at mobile; weight 800 and tight tracking. Body 16/26, hero lead 18/28, supporting labels 12–14. Existing square sentence stops on campaign headlines.
- Layout: 1152px content width, centered hero, full-width benchmark slab, smaller process band, asymmetric expert section, three aligned package columns, black closing section. Most content is left aligned after the hero.
- Surfaces: square corners, flat fills, no elevation. The benchmark is the memorable object. Its figures have clear units, ranges, and an example label.
- Motion: none needed to sell this concept. In implementation, only controls and disclosures respond to interaction; respect reduced motion.

## Editorial decisions

1. Lead with the buyer's need, then explain the benchmark and consulting offer in plain English. Explain EHS once; do not make AI the headline.
2. The product preview demonstrates what the buyer receives. Numbers reuse the repository's fictional Muster AG example; they are not customer results or independently validated benchmark evidence. The preview does not imply its three KPIs sum to the cost estimate.
3. Show estimated annual incident cost and its range. Do not promise savings or compliance. Avoid a chart that implies performance improved over time: there is no time series here.
4. Say an account is required, matching the company field's real sign-up destination. The prototype's benchmark buttons open a design-preview note; they do not submit data.
5. Remove same-week delivery and automatic shortlist promises. The current copy contradicts itself on delivery timing, and automated matching is planned. Describe coordination with the team instead.
6. Replace the six-object campaign wall with the existing steelworks photograph and a short explanation of expertise. Keep playful campaign material for About, where it does not interrupt the buying journey. This is a proposed homepage editorial change to the current design reference.
7. Keep all three approved prices and the remote versus on-site distinction. The longest package title is shortened for scanning, with the included system and culture work stated explicitly. Confirm naming before copying it into the catalogs.
8. No invented client logos, testimonials, certification badges, network membership counts, turnaround promises, or performance claims. The public SGAS directory is not evidence that every listed person is an SME24 expert.

### FAQ
**Questions, answered.**

**What do I get for free?** A benchmark based on available company information and peer data, with an estimate of annual incident costs when the required inputs are available. You can add your own figures. Expert assessments are paid packages.

**What if our safety figures are not public?** Add the figures you have in your account. Missing information stays visible so you can see what the benchmark can and cannot tell you.

**Does the benchmark confirm compliance?** No. It is a starting point for assessing performance and priorities. A compliance assessment examines your situation against the standards included in the package.

**Do I have to book an assessment?** No. You can use the free benchmark without buying a package. If you want an expert assessment, the scope and price are set out before you book.
